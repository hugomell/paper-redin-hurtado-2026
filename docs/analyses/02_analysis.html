Threat indicators

Variable Score Item description
Life experience 1 Zoo (born in the former zoo and spent their life in captivity)
2 Trade (rescued from poaching)

Deprivation indicators

Variable Score Item description
Type of rearing 1 Parenting (reared by biological parents or surogate mother/father
2 Hand (reared by humans)
Infancy social exposure 1 Accompanied by peers 80% infancy
2 Mixed or accompanied by peers 80-20% infancy
3 Alone more than 80% infancy
Juvenile social exposure 1 Accompanied by peers 80% juvenility
2 Mixed or accompanied by peers 80-20% juvenility
3 Alone more than 80% juvenility
Nutrition deprivation 1 Balanced (mostly balanced during life, adequate food items)
2 Unbalanced (poor, harmful foods like soda or snacks…)
Mother separation 1 No
2 Yes

NB: Mother separation is not used as an unpredictability indicator but a deprivation indicator contrary to what was declared in the preregistration.

Unpredictability indicators

Variable Score Item description
Number of relocations 1 born at the center
2 relocated at least once (to the rescue center)
3 relocated at least twice (usually market + rescue center)
4 relocated more than twice (more than two environments before center)

Psychopathology

The indicators for anxiety, depression and ISS mix behavioural observations with items from the Macaques Social Responsiveness scale. The latter is rated on a 1-5 Likert scale and ratings from 3 observers are averaged. Some items are reversed scored but were already recoded in the original source file DataRecoded.xslx sent by Mikel. See F1000research paper.
